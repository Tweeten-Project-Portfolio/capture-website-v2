Design List

Our plastic designs are made from 100% post-consumer plastic. This means they are made from plastic that has already been purchased and/or used by consumers, instead of being sourced from virgin plastic (or, plastic made straight from a plastic manufacturing factory). By reusing and diverting plastic that would otherwise spend hundreds of years sitting in a landfill, we not only reduce our dependency on crude oil to make new plastic, but we also prevent plastic from polluting our environment and bodies. We are proud to offer custom designs that add value to YOUR life from something as humble a plastic container.
Wallace - #2 HDPE

The Kitchen Sink - #2 HDPE
